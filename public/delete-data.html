Delete Your Data

MenuPilot Owner — restaurant order & menu dashboard, by MenuPilot Owner

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to uyentu2991@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the staff account email you use to log in to MenuPilot Owner, and the restaurant/store name.
  3. We verify the request is coming from the account owner (or the restaurant's owner, for staff accounts they manage).
  4. We delete the account within 7 business days and confirm by email once done.

What gets deleted

Your staff login (email) and authentication credentials are permanently deleted from our system (Supabase Auth) and removed from the restaurant's staff list, revoking all access immediately.

What may be retained

Order, menu, table, and reservation records are business records belonging to the restaurant, not to an individual staff account — deleting your personal staff login does not delete those records, since they may still be needed by the restaurant for accounting or operational purposes. If a restaurant owner wants their store's full data (menu, past orders, reviews) permanently deleted as well, they can request this in the same email — it will be erased within 30 days unless local law requires longer retention of transaction records.
